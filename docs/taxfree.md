#Massachusetts Tax Free Weekend


Sale of most* retail items will be exempt from the 6.25% MA state sales tax during the weekend of Saturday, August 9th and Sunday, August 10, 2025 (not before and not after!)

Purchases can be in store, via phone, or online. The $$ transaction has to occur on the weekend, but delivery can take place later.

The sales tax holiday applies to individual retail items for personal use only. Purchases by corporations or other businesses remain taxable.

Only applicable to items that cost $2,500 or less (if you buy three $1,000 items you won’t be taxed on the total sale of $3000, but you would be fully taxed on a single $3000 item)




   <span style="font-size: 2em;">Per Mass Gov’s Sales Tax FAQ:</span>



If a customer put down a deposit on, prepaid for or otherwise promised to pay for an eligible item prior to the sales tax holiday weekend, they **cannot** cancel their prior purchase and re-book the purchase on the sales tax holiday weekend to avoid the sales tax. 

If you spend more than $2,500 on an item, the entire amount paid for the item is subject to sales tax, not just the amount that exceeds that threshold. 


As long as the price of each individual item is $2,500 or less, you can purchase as many items as you want together, tax-free, even if all the items added together cost more than $2,500.

**As long as you pay for the item(s) in full on the sales tax holiday weekend**, you can arrange for delivery or pickup of the item after the sales tax holiday weekend.   

## E-Bike Voucher specifics
If a e-bike is in stock, there is nothing different that needs to be done on tax-free weekend.

If we have to special order a e-bike and cannot immediately redeem the voucher, after we place the voucher on hold, we will need to take a payment in LS using the voucher payment option for the amount that the voucher will be applied to. Follow the same process as taking a full deposit for a special order e-bike.
